🏞 Scenic • 9 routes
Best scenic road trips include Chicago Skyline Run for long-distance city driving, Great Smoky Mountains and Lake Placid for fall foliage, Vermont Fall Loop for covered bridges and Smugglers' Notch, Kancamagus Highway for 34 miles of uninterrupted White Mountain color, Niagara Falls for winter waterfall views, Peddler's Village for holiday lights, Macy's Thanksgiving Parade for events, and Hersheypark and Lake Tobias for family theme park adventures. Each guide includes best season, drive style, and full itinerary with stops.

🍂 Fall • Peak colors
Top fall foliage road trips are Great Smoky Mountains for late fall colors down south, Lake Placid & Whiteface Mountain for peak red and gold to the summit, Vermont — Quechee Gorge, Montpelier, Waterbury (Ben & Jerry's), Stowe Gondola, Smugglers' Notch VT-108, and Burlington — for early to mid-October peak, and Kancamagus Highway (NH 112) for 34 miles of pure White Mountain foliage. All guides include exact timing windows, summit drive details, and where to stop for best views.

🎢 Family • Theme parks
Hersheypark & Lake Tobias in Pennsylvania is ideal for families — roller coasters and chocolate by day, drive-through wildlife safari nearby. Chicago also works for a longer family city trip. Find both under family and theme park guides with kid-friendly timing and ticket tips.

❄ Winter • Waterfalls
Niagara Falls in winter is the best for frozen waterfalls — the falls are partially frozen, powerful, and less crowded. The guide covers how cold it really feels, what stays open, and how to plan the winter route safely with stops.

🍁 Vermont • 350 miles
Early to mid-Oct is prime. Quechee peaks first week of Oct, Montpelier/Waterbury/Stowe/Smugglers' Notch Oct 5-15, Burlington holds till Oct 15-20. ~350 miles, 12-14 hrs with stops. Ben & Jerry's first-come in fall, Stowe Gondola 10am-4:30pm no reservation. VT-108 through the Notch closes mid-late Oct after first snow — check 511VT, no RVs, 20mph hairpins. If closed, take VT-100 via Morrisville.

🍁 Kancamagus • 280 mi loop • 34 mi byway
Kancamagus Highway is NH Route 112, 34.5 miles through White Mountain National Forest from Lincoln to Conway, elevation 2,855 ft at Kancamagus Pass. Boston to Lincoln is ~2h15m. The Kanc itself is 1h non-stop but plan 3-5h with stops: Lower Falls (swimming), Albany Covered Bridge, Sabbaday Falls 0.8 mi round-trip, Sugar Hill Overlook, Hancock Overlook, CL Graham Wangan Overlook. No gas, no food, no cell service on the byway — fill in Lincoln or Conway. Best fall color late Sept to mid-Oct. Parking $5 day pass at most stops.
